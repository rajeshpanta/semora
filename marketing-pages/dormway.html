Semora vs DormWay: which syllabus planner is right for you?

Semora and DormWay both use AI to turn an uploaded syllabus into a semester timeline, but Semora adds Progress Insights, an AI-generated study schedule, and a workload dashboard behind an optional $3.99/month Pro tier, while DormWay pairs syllabus parsing with read-only Canvas, Blackboard, and Moodle sync and is currently free.

What each app does

Semora turns a course syllabus into an organized semester. You import a syllabus by photo (camera, up to 5 pages), PDF upload, drag-and-drop on the web, or by pasting raw text copied from a PDF or LMS page on the web. OpenAI GPT-5.6 Luna then extracts the course name, instructor, meeting times, office hours, semester dates, grading scale, and every assignment, exam, quiz, project, and reading with its due date — building your deadlines, grades, and class schedule automatically. Semora is available on iPhone and web, sharing one account so edits sync in near real time.

DormWay ingests syllabi (uploaded in-app or emailed to syllabus@dormway.app) and uses AI to extract assignments, exam dates, grading breakdowns, and late-work policies. It pairs that with read-only Canvas, Blackboard, and Moodle sync, merging both sources into one semester timeline and week view. DormWay also includes a GPA/grade calculator, an “Ace” AI assistant that answers policy questions with citations back to the syllabus, and a per-course “Intelligence” tab (difficulty rating, weekly hour estimate, grading policy, late-work rules). It's available on web, iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Where Semora might fit better

  • You want Progress Insights (trend charts and CSV export) layered on top of your grade tracking.
  • You want an AI-generated study schedule (Smart Plan) or a workload dashboard that flags crunch weeks and exam-heavy stretches.
  • You'd use flashcards, a Pomodoro-style focus timer, or an AI tutor chat that already knows your syllabus.
  • You want Canvas sync without waiting on an OAuth app-review process — Semora connects with a personal-access token you generate yourself.
  • You want to share a course with classmates (Course Spaces) with deadlines and group assignments syncing in real time.

Where DormWay might fit better

  • Your school uses Blackboard or Moodle rather than Canvas — DormWay's read-only sync covers all three.
  • You want a fully free product with no subscription tier at all.
  • You use an iPad or Mac app specifically, rather than a mobile-plus-web setup.

FAQ

Does Semora or DormWay sync with Canvas?

Both do. Semora connects via a personal-access token you generate yourself inside Canvas, so there's no OAuth app-review wait. DormWay offers read-only sync with Canvas, Blackboard, and Moodle, merged into one timeline.

Is Semora or DormWay free?

Semora's free tier includes 5 syllabus scans per month, up to 4 courses per semester, full task/deadline tracking, grade tracking, calendar sync, and same-day reminders; Pro is $3.99/month or $19.99/year for unlimited scans and courses plus study-planning tools. DormWay is currently free with no paid tier, per its own pricing page and App Store listing.

How do Semora and DormWay handle grade tracking?

Semora includes grade tracking in its free tier, with Progress Insights (trend charts, CSV export) and Academic Risk alerts available in Pro. DormWay's GPA/grade calculator also supports weighted categories, letting you adjust weights and test grade scenarios.

Does either app work on Android or iPad?

Neither app has an Android version — DormWay states this explicitly on its own blog, and Semora is built for iPhone and web. DormWay is also available on iPad and Mac; Semora is currently iPhone and web only, with no dedicated iPad or Mac app.
