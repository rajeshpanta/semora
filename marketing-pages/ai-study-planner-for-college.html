How Semora builds your plan

Instead of manually copying dates out of a PDF, you give Semora the syllabus itself and it does the reading.

  1. Import the syllabus. Take a photo (multi-page, up to 5 pages), upload a PDF, drag a file onto the web app, or paste raw text copied from a PDF or your school's LMS page.
  2. AI extraction. Semora uses OpenAI GPT-5.6 Luna to read the document and pull out the course name, instructor, meeting times, office hours, semester dates, grading scale, and every assignment, exam, quiz, project, and reading along with its due date.
  3. The semester is assembled automatically. Deadlines populate your calendar, grade categories populate your grade tracker, and class meeting times populate your schedule — without you typing in a single date by hand.

The complete planning system

Extracting the dates is the starting point. The planning system is what turns those dates into a schedule you can actually follow.

Smart Plan

Smart Plan (Pro) is an AI-generated, timed study schedule built from your actual deadlines. As due dates get added, moved, or completed, the plan adapts rather than staying static.

Workload dashboard

The Workload dashboard (Pro) gives you a crunch-week and exam-density view across all of your courses at once, so you can see which weeks are overloaded before you're in the middle of them.

Deadline and grade tracking

Full task and deadline tracking, plus grade tracking, are included on the free tier — along with a calendar with device sync and .ics export, and same-day reminders.

Canvas sync

If your school uses Canvas, you can connect it using a personal access token you generate yourself inside Canvas — no OAuth app-review process to wait on.

Course Spaces (collaboration)

You can share a course with classmates through an invite link. Shared deadlines and group assignments then sync in real time for everyone in that Course Space.

Cross-device sync

Semora on iPhone and Semora on the web share one account and one database, so edits made on either device sync in near real time.

How this compares to planning it yourself

Most college students currently manage a syllabus one of two ways: a paper planner or spreadsheet, or a generic to-do app like a reminders list. Both work, but both put the labor of building the plan on the student.

  • A paper planner or spreadsheet requires copying every date out of every syllabus by hand, and if a professor moves a due date, you have to notice it and re-copy it yourself.
  • A generic to-do app can hold tasks, but it doesn't know your syllabus — you still type in each assignment individually, and it has no concept of study time, workload density across courses, or grading weight.
  • Semora reads the syllabus for you, so the task list, calendar, and grade tracker start populated on day one. On Pro, Smart Plan turns that task list into scheduled study sessions, and the Workload dashboard shows you which weeks across all your courses are getting overloaded — visibility a plain checklist doesn't give you.

Frequently asked questions

What does an AI study planner actually do?

An AI study planner reads your source material — in Semora's case, a course syllabus — and automatically builds the deadlines, grade categories, and schedule that you'd otherwise have to enter manually, then can generate a study schedule from that information.

How does Semora create my study schedule from a syllabus?

You import the syllabus by photo, PDF, drag-and-drop, or pasted text. OpenAI GPT-5.6 Luna extracts the course details and every assignment, exam, and reading with its due date. On Pro, Smart Plan then generates a timed study schedule from those deadlines and adapts it as dates change.

Is Semora free, and what's included in Pro?

Semora's free tier includes one AI action for the lifetime of the account, free unlimited Canvas sync plus one course you add by hand, full deadline and grade tracking, and same-day reminders (calendar sync with .ics export is Pro). Pro ($3.99/month or $19.99/year) adds unlimited scans and courses, Smart Plan, the Workload dashboard, Flashcards, a Focus timer, an AI tutor, Academic Risk alerts, Progress Insights, and Share & Streaks.

Does Semora sync with Canvas or other tools?

Semora connects to Canvas LMS using a personal access token you generate yourself in Canvas, so there's no OAuth app-review wait. Semora also supports course collaboration through Course Spaces, where classmates share deadlines and group assignments via an invite link, and it syncs across iPhone, iPad and the web on one account.
